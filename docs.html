Using CLAWCLAWX

CLAWCLAWX is a Telegram bot for real-time crypto prices, charts, market data and price alerts. It is info & education only — not financial advice, and never touches your funds or keys.

Getting started

  1. Open the bot: t.me/Clawclawx_bot
  2. Tap Start (or send /start).
  3. Try /p btc to get the Bitcoin price — that's it.
A coin can be a symbol (btc, eth, sol) or a full name (bitcoin, ethereum).

Examples

Check a price

Send /p sol → you'll get Solana's price, 24h change, market cap and 24h volume.

Set a price alert

Send /alert eth 4000. The bot remembers your target and pings you the moment ETH crosses it. It checks prices every 60 seconds in the background.

Read a chart

Send /chart btc 90 for a 90-day BTC chart, rendered in the CLAWCLAWX red theme.

Data & limits

Market data is provided by the free CoinGecko API. The free tier is rate-limited, so during very high traffic a command may take a moment or ask you to retry.

Premium ($CLAWX)

Holding $CLAWX unlocks premium alerts & signals, higher rate limits and fee discounts, and gives you a vote in governance.

FAQ

Does the bot trade for me?

No. CLAWCLAWX is information-only. It does not place orders, hold funds, or connect to your wallet or exchange keys.

Is it free?

Yes — all core commands are free with no sign-up. Premium features are unlocked with $CLAWX.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything is for information and education only. Always do your own research.
